Site Name

Carmona Hoops

Domain: carmonahoops.com (available)

Reason: The name "Carmona Hoops" clearly identifies the geographic location (Carmona, Calabarzon) and the sport (basketball/"hoops" is a common basketball term). It's short, memorable, and immediately tells visitors this is about local basketball. The informal "hoops" creates a friendly, community-oriented feel that welcomes players of all skill levels.

Site Purpose

Carmona Hoops serves as the central hub for the local basketball community in Carmona, Calabarzon. The site provides:

  • Player Directory: A searchable database of registered players with stats, positions, and team information
  • League Information: Details about active leagues including Men's, Women's, Youth, and Recreational divisions
  • Tournament Schedules: Upcoming games, tournament brackets, and event calendars
  • Player Registration: Online registration form for new players to join leagues
  • Community Stats: Real-time statistics showing total players, active teams, and league standings
  • News & Updates: Latest announcements, featured players, and basketball news

The site aims to strengthen the local basketball community by making it easier for players to connect, find teams, track their progress, and stay informed about local basketball activities.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: New Player Seeking League

Question: "I just moved to Carmona and love playing basketball. How can I find a local league that matches my skill level and age group?"

Answer: The Leagues & Registration page provides detailed information about all active leagues (Men's, Women's, Youth, Recreational) with skill level descriptions. Users can view current teams, see player rosters, and fill out the registration form to join the league that fits them best. The form allows them to specify their position preference, skill level, and whether they want to join an existing team or create a new one.

Scenario 2: Player Looking for Competition

Question: "I want to see how I stack up against other point guards in the league. Can I compare my stats with other players in my position?"

Answer: The Players Directory features a comprehensive filter and sort system. Users can filter players by position (Point Guard, Shooting Guard, Small Forward, etc.) and sort by various statistics like points per game, assists, rebounds, or steals. Clicking on any player card opens a detailed modal showing complete stats, achievements, and bio, making it easy to compare performance with other players.

Scenario 3: Parent Finding Youth League

Question: "My 14-year-old son wants to play basketball. Are there youth leagues available, and what's the registration process?"

Answer: The home page prominently displays all active leagues including the Youth League for ages 12-17. The Leagues page provides age group details, practice schedules, and tournament dates. The registration form includes an age group selector, making it easy to sign up for the appropriate division. Contact information is readily available for parents who have questions.
